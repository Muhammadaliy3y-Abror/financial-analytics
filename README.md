# UzAuto Motors Financial Performance Analysis (2022–2025)

An exploratory financial case study and data-driven analysis evaluating revenue trends, core operating margin compression, and capital structure expansion for UzAuto Motors JSC.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Overview
* **Entity:** UzAuto Motors JSC (FY 2022–FY 2025).
* **Core Anomaly:** While top-line revenues stabilized and Net Profit Margin showed bottom-line resilience in FY 2025, core Gross and Operating Margins experienced significant compression due to rising cost of goods sold (COGS) and operational overhead.
* **Capital Shift:** Total Equity expanded by 43% in FY 2025 to $1,300M, leading to Return on Equity (ROE) compression despite sustained net earnings.

## 3. Technical Methodology
This project combines core corporate finance theory with Python-based data analytics:
* Data parsed and structured from raw corporate financial filings in Microsoft Excel.
* Automated data manipulation and calculation of margins/ratios executed in Python.
* High-resolution visual output rendered using `Matplotlib` and `NumPy`.

---

## 4. Key Risk Factors & Strategic Recommendations

### Primary Risks
1. **Core Manufacturing Margin Compression:** Gross margin fell to 11.21% in FY 2025, signaling rising production costs or unit pricing pressures.
2. **Non-Operating Earnings Reliance:** Net Income resilience ($339.6M) was buoyed by non-operating income factors rather than core vehicle manufacturing margins.
3. **Equity Dilution & ROE Compression:** A 43% jump in Total Equity diluted ROE from 34.65% down to 26.12%.

### Strategic Recommendations
1. **COGS & Direct Cost Optimization:** Implement stricter supply chain cost controls to recover gross margins back toward historical levels (~14–15%).
2. **Strategic Capital Allocation:** Deploy the expanded $1,300M equity base into high-ROI capital initiatives (such as EV manufacturing localized supply lines) to restore ROE.

---

## 5. Conclusion
UzAuto Motors maintained robust top-line scale through FY 2025 ($4,100M revenue). While operating costs squeezed core product margins, net earnings proved resilient. Management's long-term focus must center on operational efficiency and effective equity deployment.
